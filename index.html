Kinetic-theory computational fluid dynamics

The physics of flow, from the lattice up.

We investigate fluid flow at micro-, meso- and macro-scales using the lattice Boltzmann method and allied particle-based schemes — compressible, multiphase, thermal, non-Newtonian and fluid–particle flows.

What we study

Research themes

Our work spans multiphysics phenomena coupled to fluid physics — particle suspensions, applied electric and magnetic fields, reactive and combustible flows, and radiative or neutron transport.

Lattice Boltzmann method

Kinetic-theory-based solvers derived from the discrete Boltzmann equation — finite-volume, TVD, MRT, cascaded and central-moment formulations on structured and unstructured meshes.

Compressible & high-speed flows

Finite-difference LBM with high-order WENO reconstruction for supersonic and high-Mach flows; work carried forward with ISRO-RESPOND toward complex geometries and re-entry problems.

Multiphase & interface dynamics

Phase-field and multigrid LBM for droplets, bubbles, pinch-off and pool boiling — accelerating steady and transient multiphase solutions.

Thermal convection

Rayleigh–Bénard and mixed convection with anisotropic thermal diffusion in tilted and complex enclosures.

Non-Newtonian rheology

Power-law and complex-fluid flows over obstacles, cavities and corrugated geometries using MRT-LBM.

Fluid–particle systems

Coupled DEM–LBM for particle suspensions, granular impact, avalanches and electro-rheology; plus experimental fluid-particle studies.
