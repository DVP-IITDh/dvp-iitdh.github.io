About the PI

Dr. Dhiraj V. Patil

Associate Professor in the Department of Mechanical, Materials & Aerospace Engineering at IIT Dharwad, working on computational fluid dynamics and kinetic-theory-based lattice Boltzmann methods.

Dr. Patil develops advanced numerical methods for complex fluid flows, with an emphasis on mesoscopic lattice Boltzmann formulations and hybrid CFD. His group's work covers compressible and high-speed flows, multiphase flow and interface dynamics, thermal convection, non-Newtonian rheology, and coupled fluid–particle systems.

Before joining IIT Dharwad he was an Assistant Professor at IIT Mandi, and held postdoctoral research positions at the University of Edinburgh, the City College of New York, and JNCASR Bangalore. He earned his Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore.

His group has been supported by sponsored research projects from agencies including ISRO (RESPOND), DST-SERB and ANRF, along with industry CSR programmes and equipment grants. He has served as a guest co-editor for special issues in Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A and the Journal of Computational Science.
