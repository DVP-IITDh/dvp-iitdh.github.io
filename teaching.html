Teaching philosophy

For undergraduates, each topic is anchored in real-life examples, the historical development of the subject, and practical applications — consolidated through assignments and small projects.

For postgraduate and graduate students, classes lean on mathematical derivation and physical reasoning. Programming courses emphasise the craft of scientific computing through extensive hands-on coding.

Flow Physics Lab

Numerical investigation of fluid flow at micro-, meso- and macro-scales, with the lattice Boltzmann method as the primary tool. Department of Mechanical, Materials & Aerospace Engineering, IIT Dharwad.
